# Speaker Protection Circuit

A relay-based speaker protection circuit designed to protect
loudspeakers from DC faults and power-on transients.

## Features

- DC offset protection
- Turn-on delay
- Speaker disconnect relay
- 12 V relay operation
- Transistor-based protection circuit

## Circuit Diagram

The circuit monitors the amplifier output and disconnects the
loudspeaker when a potentially dangerous DC voltage is detected.
